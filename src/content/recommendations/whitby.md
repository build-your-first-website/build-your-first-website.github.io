Whitby has everything I want from a day by the sea: a harbour, a beach, narrow streets and a long climb to a view.

## Don't miss

- The 199 steps up to the abbey.
- The walk along the beach to Sandsend.
- Fish and chips, eaten outside, whatever the weather.
